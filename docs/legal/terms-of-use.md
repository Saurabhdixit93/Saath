## 1. Eligibility — 18+ Minimum Age & Minor Safety

1. You must be **18 years of age or older** and legally competent to contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 to create a Saath account, grant DPDP consents, or enter into a peer-to-peer seat-swap arrangement.
2. Individuals under 18 years of age (**minors**) may not operate an account or independently agree to seat swaps. Minors may only be represented on an accompanying verified adult's booking as non-identified party members (`age_band = "child"`). No minor's name, exact age, or contact number is collected or displayed.

---

## 2. Final Authority of TTE & Airline Cabin Crew

1. Every swap arranged through Saath is strictly an **informal, mutual passenger courtesy arrangement**.
2. **On-board authority is final:**
   - **Indian Railways:** All berth/seat exchanges remain subject to the explicit approval and discretion of the on-board **Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE)**.
   - **Airlines:** All seat exchanges remain subject to **airline cabin crew approval** and aviation safety regulations (including **DGCA** exit-row eligibility, infant/oxygen-mask seat restrictions, and weight-and-balance requirements).
3. A Saath digital **Swap Card** is a mutual coordination record only; it does **not** amend official IRCTC PNRs, railway reservation charts, or airline passenger manifests.

2. **Prohibition on Seat Selling & Ticket Transfers:**
   - You must **never** sell, auction, solicit, offer, or accept money, UPI transfers, tips, or gifts in exchange for a seat or berth.

3. **Optional Paid Productivity Tools:** Optional paid tools (such as the Group-Organiser Trip Pass at `₹199` per trip or B2B partner API access) provide convenience features only. They **never** buy, boost, or alter seat-matching priority (`priority_seat_bump: false`).

## 5. Acceptable Use, Verification Integrity & Safety Enforcement

1. **Authentic Bookings Only:** You must only verify PNRs or boarding passes belonging to you or your accompanying booking party. Submitting fraudulent, forged, or duplicate seat claims (`409` claim conflict) triggers manual review and account restriction/ban.
2. **Respect & Privacy in Chat:** Do not harass, intimidate, or solicit co-travellers. Pre-acceptance chat automatically redacts phone numbers, UPI IDs, and external links.
3. **Women-Only Mode:** Self-identifying as female solely to bypass `women_only` swap constraints is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate permanent ban.
4. **Moderation & Enforcement:** Users can block and report misconduct at any time. Receiving **3 distinct user reports** triggers automatic account restriction (`status = 'restricted'`). Moderators may `dismiss`, `hide`, `restrict`, or `ban` accounts with an immutable `audit_log` trail.
5. **Emergency Helplines:** In case of an on-board safety or security emergency on Indian Railways, contact **Rail Madad (139)** or **RPF Helpline (182)**, or alert the on-board TTE / airline cabin crew immediately.
